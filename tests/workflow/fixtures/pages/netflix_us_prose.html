Plans and Pricing

Netflix offers a variety of plans to meet your entertainment needs.

Our most affordable way to watch, which includes some ad breaks, is US$8.99 every month.

The ad-free option that most households pick streams on two screens at once for US$19.99 every month.

The top tier adds Ultra HD and four screens for US$26.99 every month.

Depending on where you live, you may be charged taxes in addition to your subscription price.
