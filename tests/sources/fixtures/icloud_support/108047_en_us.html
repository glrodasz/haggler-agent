iCloud+ plans and pricing

Learn about iCloud+ plans and pricing in your country or region.

When you sign up for iCloud, you automatically get 5GB of free storage. If you need more iCloud storage or want access to premium features, you can upgrade to iCloud+. Plans are available with 50GB, 200GB, 2TB, 6TB, or 12TB of storage, and you can share any plan with your family.

Pricing is per month and can vary by country or region. Learn how to upgrade to iCloud+.
